Candidate frequency ordering uses jieba data (67fa2e36e72f69d9134b8a1037b83fbb070b9775), MIT; see jieba-LICENSE.txt.

Japanese conversion data is adapted from Mozc (e5f1494923a0a624d01f19c50d57bbdfa03ea5ba).
https://github.com/google/mozc
Changes: deduplication and grouping by kana reading, retaining candidate cost order.
See Mozc-LICENSE.txt and Mozc-dictionary-NOTICE.txt for Google, IPAdic/NAIST/ICOT and Okinawa dictionary terms.

These dictionaries are bundled locally. Keyboard input is never sent to these upstream projects or any conversion service.

Storage format changes: bounded first-character shards; complete readings and candidate order preserved.
